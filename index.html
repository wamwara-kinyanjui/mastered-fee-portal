How the fee portal works

  1. Register your child once with the school access code and save the security key.
  2. Once the school approves, enter the key under Fees to see the balance.
  3. Pay any amount by M-Pesa. The balance updates when Safaricom confirms.
How do I check my child's fee balance?

Open the Fees tab, enter your child's security key and tap View statement.

How do I pay school fees by M-Pesa?

After viewing the statement, enter the amount and your M-Pesa number, tap Pay with M-Pesa and enter your PIN when the prompt appears on your phone.

How do I get a security key?

Open the Register tab, enter your child's details and the school access code from the school office. The key is shown once after registration.
